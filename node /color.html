Cyan Cognitive Matrix

10 variations calibrated to balance visual processing, attention mapping, and memory retention.
1. Passive Focus & Backgrounds (Low Saturation)
1. Ambient Study Wash
hsl(190, 25%, 15%)
Low saturation, deep value. Perfect background for long reading sessions to reduce digital eye strain.
2. Container Sub-Section
hsl(185, 30%, 25%)
Slightly lighter. Organizes information by visually grouping notes or card components without demanding immediate attention.
3. Matte Tint (For Light Mode)
hsl(195, 20%, 88%)
Highly desaturated light mode background variant. Prevents the "blank white screen" glare that induces headaches during study.
2. Information Scannability & Reading (Mid Saturation)
4. High-Readability Body Slate
hsl(182, 45%, 45%)
Optimal balance for secondary texts, captions, or icons. Clear color distinctiveness without vibrating against dark backgrounds.
5. Concept Highlight Text
hsl(188, 55%, 60%)
Mid-high lightness, moderate saturation. Ideal for key terms within textbook blocks. Directs eyes naturally during review scans.
6. Structured Sidebar / Anchor
hsl(178, 50%, 40%)
Deep, slightly green-leaning cyan. Excellent for active navigation tabs or progress bars to signal context in a course.
3. Active Anchoring & Actions (High Saturation)
7. Interactive Core
hsl(180, 75%, 55%)
High saturation, mid-high lightness. Standard color for primary actionable prompts, like "Start Quiz". Highly visible.
8. Micro-Interaction Focus
hsl(180, 100%, 45%)
Max saturation, slightly deeper value. Used for hover states or active button presses. Signals precise system feedback.
9. Insight & "Aha!" Discovery
hsl(174, 90%, 65%)
Bright, highly luminous mint-cyan. Reserves visual energy to mark critical formulas, correct answers, or new unlocked insights.
10. Attention Flash
hsl(180, 100%, 75%)
Maximum luminosity. Use strictly as a 1px border or a minute dot indicator to alert the user of a mistake or an unread notification.
